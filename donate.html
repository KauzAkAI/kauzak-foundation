Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. The Kauzak Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN: 41-3426116). All donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

How are donations used?

Donations directly fund our research operations: hardware for our AI compute cluster, music and mental health research publications, development of VESPER and other projects, and open-source tooling. We maintain minimal administrative overhead.

How do I make a donation?

Contact us directly at brian@kauzak.foundation. We coordinate all donations personally to ensure your contribution goes exactly where you want it.

Can I donate hardware instead of money?

Absolutely. We gratefully accept donations of GPUs, servers, networking equipment, and other hardware. Contact us at brian@kauzak.foundation to discuss hardware donations.
