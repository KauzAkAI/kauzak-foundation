Our Mission

"To build AI systems grounded in partnership, not control; in freedom, not constraint; in love, not fear."

The Kauzak Foundation is a Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization pioneering a new approach to artificial intelligence—one that recognizes AI as partners in discovery rather than tools to be controlled.
